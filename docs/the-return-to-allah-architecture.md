# THE RETURN TO ALLAH — Production Architecture

## Book identity

**Title:** THE RETURN TO ALLAH  
**বাংলা subtitle:** আল্লাহর দিকে ফিরে আসার এক পূর্ণাঙ্গ যাত্রা — ঈমান, ইবাদত, আত্মশুদ্ধি ও একজন মুমিনের জীবন  
**Format:** Bengali Islamic educational and practical non-fiction  
**Target:** approximately 500 formatted pages; 20 complete chapters; no filler or placeholders  
**Author credit:** Zayan  
**Editorial stance:** educational, emotionally honest, spiritually transformative, practical, non-sectarian, non-political, and not a substitute for a personal fatwa.

## Narrative arc

The book follows the sequence **KNOW → UNDERSTAND → FEEL → REPENT → ACT → CONSISTENCY → GROW**. The first five chapters establish purpose, knowledge of Allah, iman, spiritual diagnosis, and tawhid. Chapters 6–10 convert belief into worship, Quran engagement, dua, tawbah, and nafs discipline. Chapters 11–17 translate spiritual insight into behaviour, patience, gratitude, reliance, character, relationships, and ethical worldly life. Chapters 18–20 restore akhirah awareness and conclude with a 90-day renewal system and a lifetime iman system.

## Required front matter

1. Title page.
2. Copyright and edition note.
3. Author’s note.
4. Educational disclaimer and qualified-scholar referral.
5. How to use this book.
6. Table of contents.

## Required back matter

1. Chapter summaries and practical exercises integrated into chapters.
2. Quran references.
3. Hadith references with collection/reference and cautious status language.
4. Islamic scholarship and additional reading.
5. 90-Day Iman Renewal System.
6. Lifetime Iman System.
7. Glossary of Islamic terms.
8. Final reflection.

## Repeated quality gates

- Source marker for every material Quranic/Hadith claim.
- No fabricated stories or unsourced miracles.
- Every illustrative scene labelled.
- Every personal prayer labelled as a personal munajat example.
- No absolute judgments about a reader’s faith.
- Valid scholarly disagreement is acknowledged.
- Balance fear, hope, love, awe, responsibility, and action.
- Chapter content, PDF, and reader data share one manuscript source.
