# THE RETURN TO ALLAH — Source Ledger

## Editorial rules

This is an educational Islamic non-fiction work, not a fatwa. Quran translations are paraphrased as “অর্থের সারাংশ” unless a quoted translation is explicitly sourced. Hadith are used only with a named collection and reference; no unattributed popular sayings are treated as hadith. Legal, marital, inheritance, financial, and worship-detail questions are framed as matters for a qualified scholar where valid disagreement exists. Illustrative scenes are labelled as fictional examples and are never presented as Islamic history.

## Scholarship and further reading

The finished book will distinguish primary revelation from explanatory scholarship. Where a topic has recognized disagreement, it will be flagged rather than settled without qualification. The final bibliography will include the Qur’an, the collections above, and carefully selected classical/contemporary educational works; it will not claim a scholar said something without a traceable work and page/section where available.

## Quality-control checklist

1. Remove every placeholder and incomplete chapter.
2. Check every Quran citation for surah and ayah.
3. Check every Hadith citation against the linked collection page.
4. Do not call a narration sahih unless the source collection/reference supports that label.
5. Label illustrative scenes as fictional examples.
6. Mark personal reflective prayers as personal munajat examples.
7. Flag fiqh and personal legal questions for a qualified scholar.
8. Audit repetition, coercive fear language, sectarian attacks, political propaganda, and unsupported miracles.
9. Preserve a balanced combination of knowledge, hope, awe, accountability, and action.
10. Ensure the final PDF and reader data derive from the same audited manuscript.
