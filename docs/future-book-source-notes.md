# আগামী পৃথিবী — source notes

## Evidence handling rule

The manuscript will distinguish **KNOWN**, **EMERGING**, **PLAUSIBLE**, **SPECULATIVE**, and **UNCERTAIN** claims. Future scenarios will be labelled as scenarios rather than stated as guaranteed outcomes.

## Verified source notes

### NASA Artemis / Moon-to-Mars
NASA describes Artemis as a sequence of increasingly difficult lunar missions intended to support scientific discovery, economic benefits, and the foundation for possible crewed Mars missions. NASA’s official page reports that Artemis II completed a crewed lunar flyby in April 2026 and that NASA continues to target a first Artemis lunar landing in 2028. These are official program plans and milestones, not proof that humans will definitely live on Mars.

Source: [NASA, Moon to Mars | NASA’s Artemis Program](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/artemis/)

### IPCC climate synthesis
The UN Digital Library record for the IPCC AR6 Synthesis Report states that the report integrates the state of knowledge on climate change, widespread impacts and risks, mitigation, and adaptation. Climate chapters will separate observed conditions, projected outcomes, response options, and uncertainty.

Source: [Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change, Climate Change 2023: AR6 Synthesis Report — Summary for Policymakers](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4008082?ln=en)

### WHO ageing and health
WHO reports that the global population aged 60 and over is projected to grow from 1 billion in 2020 to 1.4 billion by 2030 and 2.1 billion by 2050. WHO also explains ageing as the accumulation of molecular and cellular damage over time, while emphasizing that healthspan depends substantially on physical and social environments. The manuscript will maintain the distinction between longer life, healthspan, and immortality.

Sources: [WHO, Ageing and health](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/ageing-and-health) and [WHO, World report on ageing and health](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241565042)

## Editorial limitations

The book should not invent statistics, research, quotations, consensus, or specific future dates. Current claims should be checked again immediately before final publication because technology, policy, and mission schedules can change. References should be attached to the relevant part’s notes and sources section.
